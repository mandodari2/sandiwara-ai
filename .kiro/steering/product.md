# Product Steering — Sandiwara AI

## What this product is

Sandiwara AI resurrects Indonesian radio drama (sandiwara radio) with AI. A dead broadcast format, reborn as a web app where anyone can generate, listen to, and share audio dramas.

## Product rules

- The listener experience comes first: one tap to play, no account needed to listen.
- Every drama must feel like radio drama, not an audiobook: distinct voices, sound effects, music.
- Indonesian stories first. Pre-loaded content is Indonesian classics (Tutur Tinular, Mahabharata, local legends).
- Language: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) for UI and generated dialogue.
- A failed generation step must never lose completed work; report what failed and keep partial output.
- No paywall, no account wall for listening. Creation is free during the hackathon.
